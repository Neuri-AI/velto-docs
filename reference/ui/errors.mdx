---
title: "UI errors"
description: "Reference PSX errors raised by markup, hooks, children, and renderers."
---

`PSXError` is the base. Public subclasses include `MarkupSyntaxError`, `InvalidChildError`, `DuplicateKeyError`, `HookOrderError`, `ThreadViolationError`, `RendererCapabilityError`, and `RendererConfigurationError`.
