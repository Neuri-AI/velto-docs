---
title: "UI hooks and markup APIs"
description: "Reference use_state, use_effect, use_ref, psx, and markup transformation APIs."
---

`use_state`, `use_effect`, and `use_ref` require a component render context. `psx` parses explicit-scope markup. `transform_source`, `transform_file`, `compile_template`, and source-map APIs are advanced PSX markup tools.
